  • What counts as a describe
    One picture or link sent to the AI to write its sentence and tags. Searching, the arc, boards, and imports without describing never count.
  • What happens at the cap
    Search keeps working. Describing pauses until the next month, or you paste your own Claude or ChatGPT key, which works on every plan.
  • Why Free has no cap
    Free runs on your own key, so the AI bill is yours and Spot's servers never see the key or your files. Plus and Studio are for people who would rather not manage a key.
  • Not on sale yet
    Plus, Studio and Once open when Spot can pay for the AI on your behalf. Until then everything is free. The prices on this page are planned and may change before launch. Cancelling keeps your files: they are plain files in your folder. Terms · Privacy.
