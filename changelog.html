Every version of Spot and what changed in it, from the same log the app ships from. Installed copies check for a new version every six hours; the newest is always at the downloads page.

0.7.8 — 7 September 2026

Changed

  • Capture prompt 30 % shorter. Two rows folded away: the Screenshot / Link toggle and the ‹ 1/9 › stepper now share the top row; the note field starts as one line and grows only as you type (up to three lines); Save sits at the right of the note field instead of on its own row, with the keyboard hints as its tooltip. Thumbnail 26. The prompt is about 164 tall including the notch band (was 233).

0.7.7 — 7 September 2026

Fixed

  • Popup closed with the pointer still inside its right half. The keep-open zone was positioned using the popup window's width at the moment it was reported, which is often the narrow closed width from a frame earlier, so the zone sat off-centre and the right part of the popup counted as "outside". The core now centres the zone itself from the popup's width, and the popup re-reports whenever its window resizes.

Changed

  • Keep-open zone extends 72 px past the left, right and bottom edges of the popup and the arc (was 56), and the close delay is 450 ms (800 ms while typing). The popup stays until the pointer is clearly past it.

0.7.6 — 7 September 2026

Changed

  • Dragging onto the notch shows only the drop target. The popup's own contents (band, pill, prompt) are hidden while something is being dragged over it, so there is one thing on screen, not two. The target says "Spot later", then "Spotting…" while the picture is fetched, then "Spotted", and the capture prompt opens with the link filled in as before.

0.7.5 — 7 September 2026

Changed

  • Capture prompt, less squished. The redundant "Just captured. One line…" subtitle is gone (the note field's placeholder says it), rows have 8 px between them and the prompt starts 10 px below the notch so the Screenshot / Link toggle never collides with it. Thumbnail 30, note field 46 tall. A screenshot with no link no longer shows an empty link row.
  • Side buttons smaller and set in: 30 wide with 8 px of air, panel 276 wide. The band's height now comes from the real notch height reported by macOS, not a constant.
  • Keep-open zone bigger than the popup: the pointer may drift 56 px past any edge of the panel or the arc without closing it, and the close grace is 320 ms (700 ms while typing) instead of 180 / 500.

0.7.4 — 7 September 2026

Fixed

  • Capture prompt cut off after a drop. The panel's height was a fixed number and the content (thumbnail, note field, link, Save row) was taller than it, so the Save row fell off the bottom until you toggled Screenshot → Link → Screenshot. The panel now measures its own content and sizes itself every time the content changes (mode switch, a drop landing, typing), so it always fits. The earlier "measured to fit" numbers were taken while the panel was scaled for its open animation and were 15 % short; that is why search clipped its hint row too.
  • The plus looked huge. In capture mode the plus button filled its whole cell with white. It is now a small white dot around the icon, and the band has 6 px of air on each side so nothing sits flush against the panel's rounded corners (panel 284 wide).

0.7.3 — 7 September 2026 · installer + landing page

Added

  • A real installer. Every release now ships a macOS .pkg (Apple's Installer: Continue → Install → Spot lands in Applications) for Apple silicon and Intel, next to the dmg and the zip the updater uses. Unsigned still, so macOS 15+ asks for System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway once.
  • Stable download addresses. Each release also carries Spot-arm64.pkg, Spot-x64.pkg, Spot-arm64.dmg, Spot-x64.dmg, so …/releases/latest/download/<name> always serves the newest build. The landing page links to those; nobody has to see GitHub.
  • Landing page at https://spotappjason.github.io — hero, Download for Mac (Apple silicon, with an Intel alternative and a dmg link), how it works, install steps, footer. Plus a privacy policy and terms page, which are what Google requires before the sign-in app can be published to everyone. Hosted on GitHub Pages from the public spotappjason/spotappjason.github.io repo; ~/spot-site is the source.
  • package.json homepage = the landing page; the in-app update bar's Get it now opens the landing page's download instead of a GitHub release page.
  • Google sign-in app published (Testing → In production): with the landing page as home page, privacy and terms, Google accepted the app for any Google account. The tester list is no longer needed.
  • Release script builds the two .pkg installers one chip at a time (electron-builder collides on a temp file when it builds both at once — that is what failed the first 0.7.3 run).

0.7.1 — 7 September 2026

0.7.0 — 7 September 2026 · downloadable Spot

Added

  • Google sign-in, required to open (D11). Supabase Auth with the Google provider, PKCE flow: Spot opens a Google tab in your browser, Google comes back to a loopback listener the app holds only for the sign-in (127.0.0.1:41729–41731, or spotapp://auth/callback), the session is sealed in the macOS keychain. Spot never sees the password. auth.js, auth-config.json (Supabase URL + anon key; empty = "not configured" and the step is skipped).
  • Bring your own AI (D12): connect Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI) with an API key from your own account. The key is sealed with Electron safeStorage (keychain), validated with a zero-token models call, never handed to a renderer. Two jobs, both with a JSON schema: widen a query (up to six tags already in the vault that the query also means — shown in the meta line, dashed on the why-chips, "(a tag your AI added)" in the reasoning) and describe a picture (title + one sentence + facet tags, proposed, applied only when you press Apply; also from an inbox row, carried into File it). ai.js.
  • AI in the notch: the typed query runs instantly; if an AI is connected the arc widens a second later with "AI added subject:sneaker …".
  • Onboarding, five steps in the main window: Welcome (vault) → Sign in with Google → Connect your AI (skippable) → Where do you save things? (sources, with Pinterest · Cosmos honestly marked "no public API or MCP yet") → Hover the notch. The notch is created only once signed in and through onboarding.
  • Account tab: who you are + sign out · your AI (model picker, widening on/off, refresh models, disconnect) · updates (version, feed, check now).
  • Updates from this terminal (D13): npm run release builds dmg + zip for arm64 and x64, smoke-tests the build, publishes to GitHub Releases in a public spot-releases repo with the CHANGELOG section as release notes, tags the code. Installed copies check every six hours; an update bar announces the new version. Signed builds download and install on restart; unsigned builds open the release page (macOS won't swap an unsigned app). updater.js, scripts/release.sh, scripts/check-changelog.js — the release fails unless this file has a section for the version.
  • Tests (npm test): ai.js post-processing + fake-key rejection against the real endpoints; the whole sign-in flow against a mock Supabase (PKCE exchange, sealed session, denied sign-in, sign-out, deep link); the updater against a local feed (newer / same / older); a smoke run from source with screenshots (SPOT_SHOT, SPOT_OB_STEP, SPOT_VIEW).
  • ~/spot-app is now a git repository (baseline commit = 0.6.1). GitHub CLI installed at ~/.spot-toolchain/bin/gh.

Changed

  • Settings split: settings.json (nothing sensitive) + secrets.json (keychain-sealed). Are.na / Raindrop tokens still live in settings.json — moving them is a follow-up.
  • Build: hardened runtime + entitlements, dmg + zip targets, minimumSystemVersion 13, spotapp:// URL scheme, single-instance lock in packaged builds.
  • fileInbox accepts tags (from a describe proposal) and writes them into the sidecar.

0.6.0 — 7 September 2026

Added

  • Reasoning on every result. Each match now records *what* it matched (the tag, the synonym, or the word in the note) and can explain itself in one sentence per term. Shown in the notch's large view ("Why: …") and in the main window's detail panel ("Why it matched …"). A false search is now a specific, fixable line — a synonym to remove, a word in a note.
  • YouTube links. Drop a YouTube link on the notch → the video's thumbnail is the picture (maxres → sd → hq), the title comes from oEmbed, the link is kept. Tutorials, in other words.
  • Cluster reasons on boards. Each cluster label says why it exists ("9 of the pictures carry theme:vintage"); the board bar explains the rule (a cluster = a tag ≥ 2 results share, the query's own tags excluded, top five, the rest → "more").

Changed

  • Boards spread out. Pictures in a cluster now only brush each other (~25% overlap) — golden-angle spiral spaced at 0.78 × tile — and the ring radius is computed from the largest cluster so clusters don't collide.
  • Drop outline traces the notch. The white outline now runs down the notch's sides and along its bottom, not just around the panel.
  • Image fetches send a Referer for CDNs that insist on one.

0.5.2 — 7 September 2026

Fixed

  • Dragged link went behind the popup. The notch window sat at the "screen-saver" level, above even the OS drag image. Now at "pop-up-menu" — above the menu bar, below drag images — so what you're dragging stays on top of the notch.
  • Dropping on the notch did nothing. At rest the window ignored the mouse, and macOS picks drop targets when a drag starts, so flipping that mid-drag was too late. At rest the window is now just the 288 × 33 strip over the notch and always accepts the mouse; a drag entering it opens the panel natively (no polling), and the window grows to the panel only while open.

The plus lights up as active, the prompt opens on exactly the dropped file (thumbnail, name), the link is already in the link field, and the notch holds open until you save or esc — even if the mouse drifts away. 0.5.0 — 7 September 2026

Added

  • Drop on the notch. Drag an image or link onto the notch; it opens under the drag; drop. The resolver (resolver.js) turns it into a picture in _inbox: a direct image is downloaded; a page's og:image / twitter:image is used; otherwise the page is rendered in Spot's own hidden offscreen window and captured. Dropped files are saved as-is. The link is kept beside the picture and the capture prompt opens with it prefilled. No browser control, no external automation — everything runs inside the app. Verified live: direct image, og:image page, plain-page render, <img> drag, dropped file.

Pull saves in from elsewhere; every item lands as a block under inspiration/<folder>/<source>/ tagged source:<platform> + meta:needs-description, so it's findable at once and waits in Findability for your sentence. 0.3.0 — 7 September 2026

Added

  • Boards, Spot's own format. boards/<slug>.spot.json replaces the Obsidian .canvas. Built the way Spiral builds a moodboard — by relationship: results split into threads (the facet values they share, the query's own tags skipped; "denim" → fading · vintage · helmut-lang · runway · dolce-gabbana), each thread a cluster on a ring, pictures fanned on a golden-angle spiral so they overlap. Positions saved.
  • Boards tab (list with covers) and a board canvas in the main window: pan, wheel-zoom around the cursor, drag pictures (saves after 400 ms), thread labels, double-click → the block. The notch's *Moodboard* builds the board and opens it.
  • D10 — independent of Obsidian. No .canvas is written any more; nothing in Spot depends on Obsidian.

Changed

  • IPC: boards:list / get / save / create; notch:moodboard now returns a board slug.

0.2.2 — 7 September 2026

Fixed

  • "shoes" didn't find all the shoes. The engine matched letters, not meaning: of 15 shoe-related blocks, the word "shoes" appeared in only 2. Three fixes:
  • Stemming — singular/plural are one word now (*shoes* = *shoe*, *boots* = *boot*).
  • Synonyms map in TAXONOMY.md ("synonyms"), bidirectional, content not code: *shoes* → boots, derby-boots, mule, sneakers, converse, sandals, footwear…; *boots* → shoes. Scored at phi.synonym 0.85 and shown with ≈ on the why-chips. Seeded for shoes, lamp, denim, jacket, tee, video, photo, label, packaging — grow it whenever a search misses.
  • Prose weight 0.3 → 0.4 so a real mention in the note clears the arc's Related line for one-word searches. ("heels" dropped from the map — it was matching a boat's heel.) Result: "shoes" 2 → all 15 shoe-related blocks on the arc (2 exact, 3 synonym, 10 prose).
  • SCHEMA.md was being indexed as a block (its example frontmatter has subject:shoe) and appeared in results. Root docs (SCHEMA, README, INDEX, LOG, TAGS, OVERVIEW) now excluded.

0.2.1 — 7 September 2026

Fixed

  • No open/close animation. Cause found on the machine: macOS Reduce Motion is on (reduceMotion = 1), and a rule in notch.css honoured it by cancelling every transition. Removed — you asked for the motion. Also made it perceptible: drop-and-settle, .22 s open / .16 s close.
  • Notch stayed open too long / area too big. The keep-open area was the whole invisible window (538 × 440). The renderer now reports the visible rectangles (the 320-wide panel, plus the arc when it's showing) and only those hold it open. Grace shortened: 180 ms normal, 500 ms while typing (was 350 / 900).

0.2.0 — 7 September 2026

Fixed

  • Arc search quality. "shoes" returned 127 results (2 real, 125 *sibling-only* — blocks that merely had any subject: tag scored 0.50, above the Related cutoff of 0.40). The arc now requires at least one exact-tag or prose match. Result: "shoes" → 1 picture of 2.
  • The notch never appeared. Diagnosed by printing the window's real bounds: macOS clamped it to y: 33, below the menu bar. enableLargerThanScreen puts it at y: 0.
  • Notch stayed open while typing. Moving away now always closes it; typing earns a 900 ms grace instead of a hold. Large view still holds.

Added

  • The arc. ↩ in the pill searches in the background (main process keeps its own index); results fan out as overlapping thumbnails on an upward-concave arc under the pill. Up to 40 pictures, Related tolerance, arc = 25% of screen width. Thumbnails are 160 px JPEGs cached in userData/thumbs, served on spot://thumb/…; hover swaps to full-res and magnifies with a Dock-style cosine falloff.
  • Large view. Click a picture → the notch window grows to the screen and shows it at ~2/3 with title, note, tags. Click outside / esc → back.
  • Open all (main window with the query; ⌘↩ too) and Moodboard (writes boards/<query>.canvas embedding every picture on the arc, registers in BOARDS.md).
  • Capture kinds. Plus → *Screenshot* (newest _inbox screenshot + one sentence) or *Link* (description + required URL, no inbox needed — for videos).
  • Capture notes. Saved as _inbox/<basename>.md beside the screenshot; carried into the real sidecar when filed from the Inbox tab, which now shows the note.
  • × at the arc's right end clears the pictures. Results survive a close; × / esc clears.
  • Diagnostics: SPOT_NOTCH_TEST=1 force-opens the panel and runs a search; smoke output prints notch bounds and renderer console.

Changed

  • Notch band: vault icon (left) · notch · plus (right); search pill beneath. Wordmark removed.
  • Panel shrunk: 320 wide; 104 tall (search) / 268 (capture). Reads as the notch swelling.
  • The arc lives outside the panel — the popup keeps its size; pictures float beneath, unclipped (window sized arc + 160 wide, 440 tall). Curve eased to 55% of the first dip.
  • Open/close animation: scaleY from the top, .16 s open / .10 s close.
  • Pill placeholder: "Spot it".
  • Hover hit-zone widened to notch + side buttons (288 × 33).

Docs

  • spot/UI.md: notch v2 spec, arc spec (marked built).
  • spot/research/sources.md: where people save inspiration, per-platform reachability (API / data-export / browser-agent), mirror-vs-index analysis → hybrid. D9 opened.
  • Canvas: notch artboards in three states (rest, search, capture), republished.
