How it works

Save without stopping

Take a screenshot, or drag a picture or a link onto the notch. One sentence about what it is and why. That sentence is what finds it later.

Ask in your own words

"shoes", "walnut lamp", "that grainy Berlin poster". Spot ranks everything you own and never hides a result; widen the net when you want more.

Your AI, your account

Optionally connect Claude or ChatGPT with a key from your own account. It widens searches and describes pictures for you. Spot never sees or bills those calls.

Nothing leaves your Mac

Your references are files in a folder you choose. Spot reads them; it does not upload them. Sign-in is only so updates and, later, plans can find you.

Install

Download and open the installer.It walks you through Continue → Install. Spot lands in Applications.
If macOS says it can't check the developer.Spot isn't signed with Apple yet. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, press Open Anyway, then open Spot again. Older macOS: right-click the installer → Open.
Sign in with Google.A browser tab opens; pick your account; come back. Spot is in early access, so if Google says "Access blocked", email the address you want to use and it will be enabled.
