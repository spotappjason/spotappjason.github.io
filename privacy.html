Spot

Privacy policy

Effective 7 September 2026

Spot is a Mac application made by Jason Hong. This policy says what Spot stores, where, and why. The short version: your references never leave your computer; the only personal data Spot keeps online is what is needed to sign you in.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your references. Pictures, links and the notes you write about them are files in a folder on your computer that you choose. Spot reads and organises that folder. It does not upload it, copy it, or send it anywhere.
  • Your AI keys. If you connect Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI), the API key you paste is encrypted with the macOS keychain and stored only on your Mac. It is sent only to the provider you chose, only when you use an AI feature. Spot's servers never receive it.
  • Settings. Preferences and the sign-in session are stored on your Mac.

What is stored online

  • Your account. Signing in with Google gives Spot your Google account's email address, name and profile picture, and creates a login session. This is stored by Supabase, the authentication service Spot uses, in the United States. It is used to identify you across updates and, in the future, to attach a plan to your account. Nothing about your references is stored there.

Third parties Spot talks to

  • Google, to sign you in. Google's privacy policy applies to the sign-in step.
  • Supabase, which stores the account record above.
  • Anthropic or OpenAI, only if you connect a key, only for the pictures and search words you send them through Spot. Their policies apply to those requests.
  • GitHub, which hosts Spot's downloads. Spot checks it for new versions; that request carries no personal data.

Spot contains no analytics, no advertising and no trackers.

Deleting your data

Sign out to end the session and delete the app to remove everything stored on your Mac; your references folder is yours and stays where it is. To delete your account record, email contentforjason@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and it will be removed within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version is published at this address.
